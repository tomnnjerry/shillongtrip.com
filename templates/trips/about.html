{% icon 'spark' %} The story in our logo

A bridge that grows

Our mark is a living root bridge under a cloud. Khasi and Jaintia villages have trained the roots of rubber fig trees across streams for generations; the bridges grow stronger as they age, and some carry people across rivers today. We like that idea for travel: built slowly, with local hands, and better every year.

The cloud is for Meghalaya, the “abode of clouds”, and the sun-orange dot is for the mornings when it lifts.

{% icon 'leaf' %} Travelling well

How we try to leave it better

  • {% icon 'check' %}Village rules first. Mawlynnong, Mawphlang and Nongriat have their own rules on litter, photography and the sacred forest. We brief you before you arrive.
  • {% icon 'check' %}Go easy on root bridges. We keep groups small on the trails and follow local limits on how many people cross at once.
  • {% icon 'check' %}Local stays where they fit. Homestays in Nongriat, Shnongpdeng, Ziro and Khonoma put money straight into the village.
  • {% icon 'check' %}Wildlife on its terms. Official safari slots, quiet vehicles and no feeding or chasing animals for photos.
