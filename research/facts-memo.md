# Facts memo — Shillong Trip

Compiled 30 Sep 2026 by the research desk. Every bullet has a source and the date we accessed or the date the source was published. Confidence tags: **[V]** confirmed on an official or primary source; **[S]** secondary source (news, Wikipedia, operator); **[U]** unverified or conflicting, so write around it or leave it out.

House rule for writers: permits, Kaziranga dates, Bum La, road status, festival dates and fees change. Always add "check current status before you travel".

## 1. Permits

### Arunachal Pradesh: ILP for Indian citizens

- **New rules:** *Arunachal Pradesh Inner Line Permit Guidelines, 2026*, notified 26 Mar 2026 and published in the Extraordinary Gazette No. 89 on 31 Mar 2026. **[V]** Source: https://printing.arunachal.gov.in/uploads/pdf/69fd9724679ee_1778226980.pdf (accessed 30 Sep 2026)
  - Tourists now get a **Temporary ILP**. It is valid for **15 days** from the proposed entry date and covers **all of Arunachal**. Online applications are auto-approved after Aadhaar PIN/OTP authentication. **[V]**
  - Fee (Schedule III): **₹300 for 15 days**. Longer temporary ILPs are ₹300 up to 180 days, ₹500 for 1 year and ₹1,000 beyond 1 year. The DC decides these within 7 days. If the DC does not decide, a 30-day eILP is issued automatically. Renewal costs the same as the ILP. **[V]**
  - Extensions: apply online at least 7 days before expiry (Form D). **[V]**
  - Documents: Aadhaar e-KYC plus one photo ID (voter ID, driving licence, passport or PAN) and a recent passport photo. You must give your itinerary, places of stay, a local contact and an emergency contact. **[V]**
  - Groups: a **registered tour operator** can apply for groups of up to **50 people**. **[V]**
  - Penalty for breaking ILP conditions: ₹1,000. The DC can also bar a person from getting an ILP for up to 2 years. **[V]**
- **Conflict to handle:** the older portal info page still shows ₹100 for any ILP, 30-day provisional and 365-day regular (https://eilp.arunachal.gov.in/ilpInfo, accessed 30 Sep 2026). A tourist-eILP page and several 2025–26 blogs quote **₹300 for up to 3 days and ₹500 for more than 3 and up to 14 days** (reifytravels.com, updated 19 Sep 2026; arunachaltourism.com/ilp). **[U]** Writer line: "about ₹300 to ₹500 per person for a tourist ILP. Pay whatever the portal shows."
- **Apply:** https://eilp.arunachal.gov.in (Indian citizens only). Facilitation counters are at Naharlagun and Gumto railway stations, Guwahati LGBI Airport, Asom Paryatan Bhawan (Guwahati) and Donyi Polo Airport, Itanagar. Deputy Resident Commissioner offices in Guwahati, Tezpur, Dibrugarh, Kolkata and Delhi can also issue permits. **[S]** https://arunachaltourism.com/ilp/ (accessed 30 Sep 2026)
- **Check gates:** there are 71 check gates on the Assam–Arunachal boundary. **[S]** Arunachal Times, 11 May 2026: https://arunachaltimes.in/index.php/2026/05/11/robust-ilp-checking-mechanism-required/
  - The main tourist gates are **Bhalukpong** (for Tawang, Bomdila and Dirang), **Banderdewa** (for Itanagar and Ziro), **Kimin** (for Ziro), **Seijosa** (for Pakke), Ruksin/Pasighat, and Sunpura/Dirak (for Namsai/Tezu). **[S]**
  - Bhalukpong, Kimin and Pasighat are reported to have QR scanners. **[S]**
  - Carry the eILP (print or phone) **plus the original ID**. **[S]**

### Arunachal Pradesh: foreigners (PAP)

- Foreigners need a **Protected Area Permit**, not an ILP. They apply through an **approved tour operator**, via the MHA, the FRROs (Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai), Indian missions abroad, or the Arunachal Home Commissioner or Secretary (Tourism) in Itanagar. **[S]** https://arunachaltourism.com/protected-area-permit/ ; https://www.tourmyindia.com/states/arunachalpradesh/permit-for-foreign-tourists.html (both accessed 30 Sep 2026)
- **Group size:** 2 or more people with a pre-drawn itinerary through a recognised Indian agency. Solo travellers are reported to be allowed only for **Tawang, Bomdila and Ziro**. **[S]**
- **Fee:** the state tourism page says **USD 30** royalty per person, while TourMyIndia says **USD 50**. **[U]** Say "USD 30–50 royalty, confirmed by your operator".
- **Validity:** reported as 30 days, not extendable. **[S]**
- **Restricted nationals:** citizens of Afghanistan, China and Pakistan (and people of Pakistani origin) need prior MHA clearance. **[S]** https://www.mha.gov.in/sites/default/files/2022-08/FAQs-onPAPandRAP[1].pdf

### Nagaland, Mizoram, Manipur

- **The big change:** on **17 Dec 2024** the MHA **reimposed the Protected Area Regime** in **Manipur, Nagaland and Mizoram**. This ended the relaxation that had run since 2011. Foreigners again need a PAP in advance. **[S]** EastMojo, 19 Dec 2024: https://eastmojo.com/manipur/2024/12/19/foreigners-now-require-special-permission-to-visit-manipur-nagaland-and-mizoram/
- **Nagaland ILP:** apply at https://ilp.nagaland.gov.in. **[V]** https://ilp.nagaland.gov.in/fees (accessed 30 Sep 2026)
  - Domestic tourists pay **₹200** for 30 days. Foreigners pay **₹500** for 30 days, on top of the PAP. There is a payment-gateway surcharge.
  - Applications are online only from 31 Dec 2024. Approval typically comes within about 4 working hours. **[S]**
  - Nagaland is a dry state (FCDO). **[S]**
- **Mizoram ILP:** apply at https://ilp.mizoram.gov.in. **[S]**
  - Temporary pass is 15 days and renewable once for 15 days. The fee is about ₹120 plus UPI charges. **[U]**
  - An **on-arrival counter at Lengpui Airport** still issues temporary passes. **[S]**
  - Foreigners need a PAP in advance through e-FRRO and must **register with SP (CID/SB), Mizoram within 24 hours** of arrival. **[S]** https://northeastindiaconnect.com/how-to-apply-mizoram-ilp/ (31 Jul 2026)
  - Mizoram is a dry state. **[S]**
- **Manipur ILP:** apply at https://manipurilponline.mn.gov.in. **[V]**
  - The Temporary permit is valid for **30 days**, and there is an instant Aadhaar-OTP route. Counters are at **Mao Gate, Jiribam railway station and Imphal Airport**, and at DC offices. **[V]** https://manipurilponline.mn.gov.in/aboutIlp.aspx (accessed 30 Sep 2026)
  - The fee is reported as ₹100. **[U]**
  - Foreigners need a PAP. **[S]**

### Meghalaya and Tripura

- **Meghalaya:** there is **no ILP** for Indians or foreigners. **[S]** https://welcomemeghalaya.com/travel-info/ilp-requirement-clarification-not-required-for-meghalaya/
  - **From June 2025, every hotel, homestay, resort and private landlord must register guests on the state tourism app.** The app is linked to the police. The owner is liable if an unregistered guest commits a crime. **[S]** Shillong Times, 18 Jun 2025: https://theshillongtimes.com/2025/06/18/govt-orders-mandatory-registration-of-tourists-by-homestays-resorts/
  - **Renting private vehicles to tourists for self-drive is prohibited.** These steps followed the May 2025 Sohra murder case. **[S]** Outlook Traveller, 19 Jun 2025: https://www.outlooktraveller.com/News/heres-why-meghalaya-has-made-tourist-registration-mandatory-at-all-accommodations
  - **Planned, not yet live (as of July 2026):** mandatory **visitor registration at entry points** "on ILP lines". Registration centres are planned at Umling (on the Guwahati–Shillong road) and Berubari (Garo Hills). No fee has been announced. **[S]** Assam Tribune, 12 Jul 2026: https://assamtribune.com/north-east/meghalaya-plans-mandatory-registration-for-tourists-visitors-on-ilp-lines-1614168
  - We found **no statewide tourist entry fee**. Village and community sites charge their own small gate fees (see §8).
  - Foreigners: hotels file Form C as they do everywhere in India. **[S]**
- **Tripura:** there is no ILP for Indians, and Tripura is not a PAP state. FCDO notes communal and political clashes and India–Bangladesh border skirmishes. **[S]** https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/india/regional-risks (accessed 30 Sep 2026)

## 2. Tawang, Bum La, Sela

- **Sela Tunnel** opened in **March 2024**. It is about 12 km including approaches, at about 13,000 ft, and bypasses Sela Pass (about 4,200 m). It saves about 10 km and about 1 hour between Dirang and Tawang, and it gives all-weather access on the 171 km Bomdila–Tawang road. **[S]** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sela_Tunnel (accessed 30 Sep 2026)
  - Writer line: the old Sela Pass road and lake are still a sightseeing stop in fair weather. The tunnel is the winter lifeline.
- **Bum La Pass** is at 4,600 m (15,200 ft) on the India–China border, 37 km from Tawang. **[S]** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bum_La_Pass
  - It is open to **Indian civilians only**, with Army permission. Get a permit from the **DC office, Tawang**, then have it **stamped at the Army cantonment, Tawang**. Apply the day before through your hotel, driver or operator. **[S]**
  - Only SUVs can go, and only in clear weather. The road closes after snowfall. **[S]**
  - **Closed days:** these are set by the Army and change. We could not verify a fixed weekly closure. **[U]**
  - Write: "Bum La runs on Army permission day by day; closures after snow are common between Dec and Mar."
- **Madhuri Lake (Sangetsar Tso)** is beyond the Bum La turn-off, about 7 km past the fork. You need the same Tawang-issued permit. **[S]**
- **Tawang road (NH-13):** from Guwahati the route is Tezpur – Bhalukpong (ILP gate) – Bomdila – Dirang – Sela Tunnel – Tawang. That is about 450–555 km. **[S]** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tawang
  - Plan **2 driving days each way**, overnighting at Dirang or Bomdila. The practical minimum is **6N/7D** and the comfortable length is 7N/8D.
  - Landslides on the Bhalukpong–Bomdila stretch are routine in Jun–Sep. We did not verify a specific 2025–26 closure. **[U]**

## 3. Wildlife parks

### Kaziranga (Assam)

- **2025–26 season:** the **Bagori (Western) limited circuit reopened 26 Sep 2025**, ahead of Durga Puja. **Kohora (Central) opened in the 2nd week of Oct 2025.** **Elephant safaris resumed 1 Nov 2025.** **[S]** Assam Tribune, 24 Sep 2025: https://assamtribune.com/assam/kaziranga-national-park-to-reopen-for-tourists-from-sept-26-ahead-of-durga-puja-festivities-1592479
- **2026–27 season:** as of **24 Sep 2026**, the DFO expected reopening **in October 2026**. Road repairs in Burapahar and Western ranges were slowed by standing water. **[S]** https://assamtribune.com/amp/assam/kaziranga-readies-for-october-reopening-but-water-slows-road-repair-work-1618348
- **Normal pattern:** the park opens Oct (in phases) and closes for the monsoon from about May. The best months are mid-Nov to Apr. Write "Oct/Nov to Apr–May, dates set each year". **[S]**
- **Zones:** Central (Kohora), Western (Bagori), Eastern (Agaratoli) and Burapahar (Ghorakati). Elephant rides run only in Kohora and Bagori. **[S]**
- **Timings (typical):**
  - Jeep: morning about 7:30 am to midday, afternoon about 1:30 pm to about 3:30–5 pm. Each slot is usually about 2 hours on track.
  - Elephant: about 5:30–7:30 am, in 1-hour batches.
  - **[S]** Nezo Tours (Sep 2026): https://www.nezotours.com/destinations/india/assam/kaziranga/experiences/jeep-safari ; AssamInfo
- **Fees:**
  - Entry is **₹100 per Indian adult** and **₹650 per foreign adult** (plus a small service charge). Two independent sources agree. **[S]**
  - Camera: still **₹100 Indian / ₹200 foreign**, video ₹1,000, professional ₹1,000 / ₹3,000. Mobile phones are free. **[S]**
  - **Jeep cost per vehicle (up to 6 people) varies widely by source.** It is about ₹3,000–5,500 for Indians and about ₹6,500–9,000 for foreigners, including the road toll, driver and guide. **[U]** Publish as "from about ₹3,500 per jeep".
  - Elephant ride: about ₹1,200–2,000 per Indian per seat, and more for foreigners. **[U]**
- **Online booking:** we could not confirm an official public jeep-booking portal. kaziranga.assam.gov.in was unreachable on 30 Sep 2026. In practice, jeeps are booked through the range office queue, lodges or operators. Elephant seats are limited, so book them early. **[U]**
- **Night safari / new rules:** there is no verified official launch. Do not publish. **[U]**

### Other parks

- **Manas NP** (Bansbari/Barpeta Road; ranges Panbari, Bansbari, Bhuyanpara). It **reopened 24 Sep 2025**. The season is typically Oct–May. **[S]** Assam Tribune, 24 Sep 2025; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manas_National_Park
- **Pobitora WLS** (Morigaon) has Assam's second-largest rhino population and is about 1–1.5 h from Guwahati. It has jeep and elephant rides. The season is about Nov–Apr. **[S/U]** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pobitora_Wildlife_Sanctuary
- **Nameri NP** is 35 km from Tezpur, entered at Potasali. It has walking trails with an armed guard and Jia Bhoroli rafting. The season is about Nov–Apr. **[S/U]** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nameri_National_Park
- **Dibru-Saikhowa NP** (Tinsukia) has boat safaris, feral horses and river dolphins. The season is about Nov–Apr. **[U]**
- **Namdapha NP** (Changlang district) is entered through **Miao**, and the tourist zone is Deban. Indians need an Arunachal ILP. Foreigners need a PAP (Changlang/Miao–Namdapha circuit) and a park entry permit from the Field Director, Miao. The best time is Oct–Apr, with snow above 2,700 m in Dec–Mar. **[S/U]** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Namdapha_National_Park

## 4. Getting there

- **Guwahati (GAU), LGBI Airport:** 28 km from Guwahati city. **[S]** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lokpriya_Gopinath_Bordoloi_International_Airport (accessed 30 Sep 2026)
  - The **new terminal opened 20 Dec 2025**, with capacity for 13.1 m passengers a year. It is Adani-operated.
  - International flights: Bangkok-DMK (Thai AirAsia) and Paro/Singapore (Drukair). **IndiGo to Bangkok-BKK starts 27 Oct 2026.**
- **Shillong (SHL), Umroi:** 30 km from Shillong, with a 1,829 m runway (small aircraft). **[S]** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shillong_Airport
  - **IndiGo** flies to Dibrugarh, Imphal and Kolkata. **Alliance Air** flies to Aizawl, Dimapur, Guwahati, Lilabari and Tezpur. **SpiceJet** flies to Delhi.
  - Traffic was 96,716 passengers in FY 2025–26, down 19.4%. A 900 m runway extension is planned.
  - Writer line: "most visitors still fly into Guwahati".
- **Itanagar, Donyi Polo Airport (Hollongi, HGI):** 14 km south of Itanagar, opened Nov 2022. IndiGo flies to Delhi, Kolkata and Mumbai, and Alliance Air to Guwahati and Tezu. **[S]** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donyi_Polo_Airport
- **Other gateways (general knowledge, routes change):** **[U]**
  - Dibrugarh (DIB) for upper Assam and Namdapha.
  - Jorhat (JRH) for Majuli and Kaziranga East.
  - Tezpur (TEZ) for Nameri and the start of the Tawang road.
  - Dimapur (DMU) for Kohima, about 3 h away.
  - Imphal (IMF).
  - Agartala (IXA).
  - Lengpui (AJL) for Aizawl, about 30 km away.
- **Helicopters:**
  - The Pawan Hans booking portal (https://booking.pawanhans.co.in, accessed 30 Sep 2026) lists NE sectors only for **Imphal, Jiribam and Tamenglong**. It shows **no Guwahati–Shillong or Guwahati–Tawang sector**. **[V]**
  - A Guwahati–Tawang biweekly service run by the Arunachal government started in Oct 2014. **[S]** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tawang
  - **We could not verify any current Guwahati–Shillong or Guwahati–Tawang schedule or fare.** **[U]**
  - Do not sell helicopter transfers. Mention them only as "subject to operation; check current status".
- **Road times:**
  - **Guwahati–Shillong** is about 100 km on NH-6 and takes 2.5–3.5 h. **GAU airport–Shillong** takes about 3–3.5 h. Umiam Lake is 15 km before Shillong. **[S/U]**
  - A Guwahati–Silchar (Panchgram) expressway was announced in Feb 2025. **[S]** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umiam_Lake
  - **Guwahati–Tawang** takes 2 days each way (see §2).

## 7. Advisories (neutral wording)

- **Manipur:**
  - UK FCDO advises **against all but essential travel to Manipur**. It cites insurgent groups and ethnic violence from 2023, with intermittent incidents through May–July 2025, and warns of possible curfews, transport disruption and internet shutdowns. **[S]** https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/india/regional-risks (accessed 30 Sep 2026)
  - President's Rule ran from 13 Feb 2025 to 4 Feb 2026, when a new Chief Minister took office. Wikipedia notes new Kuki–Naga tension from Feb 2026. **[S]** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023–2025_Manipur_violence
  - House position: we do not sell Manipur packages. We describe it as "check the current advisory and local conditions".

- **Roads:**
  - NH-6 (Guwahati–Shillong–Jowai–Silchar) and NH-13 (Bhalukpong–Tawang) both see **monsoon landslides (Jun–Sep)**.
  - On NH-6, the Sonapur stretch (Jowai–Silchar) is the known trouble spot.
  - We did not verify specific 2025–26 closure notices. **[U]** Always add "check current status before you travel".

## 8. Entry fees at key sights

- **Kamakhya Temple, Guwahati:** **[V]** https://www.maakamakhya.org (accessed 30 Sep 2026)
  - The general queue is **free** and can be booked online at mkdonlinegenq.in.
  - **Special darshan costs ₹501 per person** (mkdonline.co.in).
