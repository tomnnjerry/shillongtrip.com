Most Tawang budgets go wrong on one assumption: that the drive is a transfer. It is not. Guwahati to Tawang is roughly 450–550 km of mountain road, depending on route, over two days each way, which means the car is with you for the whole trip and costs more than your hotels. Price the car properly and a Tawang trip becomes easy to plan.

This is how the Shillong Trip Travel Desk costs a [Tawang](/places/tawang/) trip for 2026: vehicle rates, the Bum La taxi, permits, hotels, meals and three worked examples. All figures are indicative ranges for Indian travellers, twin sharing, starting and ending in Guwahati.

## Where the money goes

For a couple on a standard 6-night trip, the split looks roughly like this:

| Cost line | Approx. share | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Private SUV, 7 days | 40–45% | Guwahati–Bomdila–Tawang–Dirang–Guwahati |
| Hotels, 6 nights | 25–30% | Bomdila or Dirang, then Tawang |
| Meals | 15–18% | Hotel dinners, momo and thukpa lunches |
| Bum La local vehicle and permit | 6–8% | Separate from your SUV |
| ILP, entry fees, tips | 3–5% | Small but unavoidable |

Change the group size and the table changes. Six people in one Innova pay a sixth of the car each, and hotels and food become the larger lines.

## Vehicle rates for the Tawang loop

Almost every Tawang trip runs on a hired SUV with driver from Guwahati or Tezpur. Arunachal hill rates are higher than plains rates because of the terrain, fuel use and empty return days.

| Vehicle | Seats | Per day (indicative 2026) | 7-day Guwahati loop |
|---|---|---|---|
| Innova Crysta | 6–7 | ₹5,000–6,500 | ₹35,000–45,000 |
| Xylo / Scorpio | 6 | ₹4,500–5,500 | ₹32,000–38,000 |
| Bolero / Sumo | 7–8 | ₹4,000–5,000 | ₹28,000–35,000 |
| Dzire / Etios (not advised) | 4 | ₹3,500–4,500 | ₹25,000–30,000 |
| Shared Sumo seat, Tezpur or Guwahati–Tawang | 1 | ₹900–1,600 one way | — |

Day rates include fuel and the driver's allowance. Some drivers charge a small night halt; ask. Starting from Tezpur instead of Guwahati saves about 180 km each way and usually one day of hire. ## Starting from Tezpur instead

[Tezpur](/places/tezpur/) sits on the north bank of the Brahmaputra, about 180 km closer to Tawang than Guwahati, and has its own small airport with limited flights. Starting and ending there cuts roughly a day of car hire off the loop, which saves ₹5,000–6,500 on an SUV. The catch is that Tezpur flights are fewer and pricier than Guwahati's, so the saving on the car can disappear into the air fare. For a group of five or six, flying into Guwahati and taking a bus or shared taxi to Tezpur often works out best. For a couple on a short break, the simplest plan is still a Guwahati car both ways with a night at Bomdila or Dirang each direction.

## The Bum La day

Your Guwahati car will not take you to [Bum La Pass](/places/bum-la-pass/). The road beyond Tawang is open only to local Arunachal-registered vehicles, usually Sumos, Scorpios or Boleros, arranged through a taxi counter or your hotel. For the full day including [Madhuri Lake](/places/madhuri-lake/) and PTSO Lake, expect about ₹5,000–8,000 per vehicle for up to five or six people. Solo travellers can sometimes buy a shared seat for roughly ₹1,000–1,500.

The Bum La permit is issued by the Deputy Commissioner's office in Tawang and then stamped at the Army cantonment, usually the day before, against your ILP and photo ID. Hotels, drivers and operators handle the paperwork, sometimes for a small service charge. Bum La runs on Army permission day by day, is open to Indian nationals only, and closures after snow are common between December and March. Check current status before you travel. Our [Bum La permit guide](/guides/bum-la-pass-permit/) has the step-by-step.

## Permits and fees

- **Arunachal Temporary ILP for Indian visitors:** applied online with Aadhaar authentication and valid 15 days across the state under the 2026 guidelines. Budget about ₹300–500 per person and pay what the portal shows. Carry it with your original ID for the Bhalukpong check gate. See our [Arunachal ILP guide](/guides/arunachal-ilp-guide/).
- **Protected Area Permit for foreign nationals:** arranged through an approved operator, usually for two or more people, with a royalty of about USD 30–50 per person; Bum La is not open to foreigners.
- **Tawang Monastery museum, war memorial, Nuranang Falls:** small entry or camera fees, typically under ₹100 per person.
- **Parking and road charges:** a few hundred rupees across the trip.

Permit fees are set by the state and change without much warning, so check current status before you travel.

## Meals

Food is cheap and filling. A plate of momos or thukpa costs ₹100–200 in Tawang market; a thali ₹200–350; a hotel dinner ₹400–800 per head. Budget per person per day:

- Budget: ₹600–900
- Standard: ₹1,000–1,500
- Premium and luxury: ₹1,500–2,500

On the drive days, lunch is at roadside dhabas in Bhalukpong, Bomdila, Dirang or Jang. Carry snacks for Sela, where options are limited in winter.

## Why advertised prices look lower

You will see 6-night Tawang packages advertised from about ₹24,000–30,000 per person. That is not out of line with our numbers once you compare the same things. Those "from" prices usually cover the shared cost of the car and a standard room with breakfast, twin sharing, and nothing else. In our couple example below, car and rooms alone work out to about ₹29,500 per person. Dinners, lunches on the road, the Bum La vehicle, permits, heaters and tips are what take the all-in figure higher.

Before you pay a deposit, ask three questions: is the Bum La day included or extra, is the car exclusive to you or shared with another group, and are the Tawang nights in town or 10–15 km out on the Bomdila road?

## Costs people forget

Tawang budgets are usually short in the same places:

- **The spare day.** Snow at Sela in winter, landslides between Bhalukpong and Bomdila in the monsoon, or a Bum La closure can cost you a day. A spare night in Tawang or Dirang adds a room and a day of car hire, about ₹8,000–10,000 for a couple.
- **Heaters and hot water.** From November to March, rooms without central heating charge extra for heaters, and some budget hotels charge for extra buckets of hot water.
- **The driver's stay.** Most day rates include the driver's food and lodging. A few do not; ask before you confirm.
- **Warm clothing.** If you do not own a down jacket, gloves and snow boots, renting or buying in Tawang market costs a few hundred to a couple of thousand rupees.
- **A Guwahati night.** Flights that land after noon rarely allow a same-day start. A night in Guwahati costs ₹1,800–4,000 for a decent room.
- **Oxygen and medicines.** Bum La is at about 4,600 m. A small oxygen can and basic altitude medicine, taken on medical advice, cost little but matter.

## Package or do it yourself

Solo travellers who are happy with shared Sumos, early starts and simple guesthouses can do Tawang independently for well under a package price. The ILP is online, shared vehicles leave Tezpur and Guwahati daily, and Tawang guesthouses take walk-ins outside peak weeks.

For couples and families, a package usually costs about the same as arranging it yourself and removes the hardest parts: finding a reliable driver who knows the Sela road in winter, arranging the Bum La permit and local vehicle the day before, and rerouting when a road closes. We also think the car is where cutting corners is least wise on this route. Whatever you book, insist on an SUV, a driver with recent Tawang experience, and a written plan for a lost day.

## When costs rise and fall

Peak demand in Tawang comes in two blocks: April to June, when summer holidays and clear weather bring families, and October to November, when skies are sharpest and festivals run. Expect hotel rates 15–30% above our tables and fewer free cars; book 6–8 weeks ahead.

December to February brings snow at [Sela Pass](/places/sela-pass/) and Bum La. Rates can be reasonable, but budget one spare day for closures and pay for heaters. July to September is the cheapest period, with discounts from many hotels, but landslides can hold you on the road for hours. Check current road status before you travel.
