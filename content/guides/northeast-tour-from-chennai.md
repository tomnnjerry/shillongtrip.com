Chennai and Meghalaya get their big rains at opposite ends of the year. When Chennai is under the northeast monsoon in November, Shillong is clear, cool and at its best. That single fact makes Meghalaya one of the most natural holidays for Chennai travellers.

We plan many trips for families and couples from Chennai. This guide covers flights, how many days to take, what to eat, what to pack and the routes that work best.

## Chennai and Meghalaya: opposite seasons

| Months | Chennai | Meghalaya | Verdict for a Chennai trip |
|---|---|---|---|
| October to December | Northeast monsoon, the city's wettest months | Dry, clear, green after the rains | The best match of all |
| January to February | Pleasant, mild | Cold and dry, crystal-clear Umngot river | Excellent; pack woollens |
| March to May | Hot, often above 35°C | Warm days, cool nights, storms from April | A cool escape in the school holidays |
| June to September | Hot with some showers | Heavy southwest monsoon | Only if you want full waterfalls and rain |

The practical message: if Chennai's own rains are keeping you indoors, it is exactly the right time to be in Shillong.

| Option | Time | Frequency | Notes |

Schedules change by season, so check current status before you travel.

### Flights

The Chennai to Guwahati non-stop is shorter than most people expect: about 2 hours 15 to 2 hours 25 minutes, less than the Mumbai or Bangalore flights to the same airport. The catch is frequency. There are fewer non-stops a day than from Delhi or Mumbai, and they fill up in holiday weeks.

Booked four to six weeks ahead, we typically see one-way economy fares of about ₹7,000 to ₹10,000 per person, rising around Pongal, Christmas and summer holidays. These are indicative. If non-stop fares jump, a one-stop via Kolkata is often cheaper and still gets you to Guwahati the same day.

From Guwahati airport, Shillong is about 120 km, roughly 3 to 3.5 hours by road. An arrival before 1 pm gets you to Shillong in daylight in most months. Our [how to reach Shillong guide](/guides/how-to-reach-shillong/) explains the road, shared taxis and the small Shillong airport at Umroi.

### Trains

Trains linking the Chennai area with Guwahati take roughly 50 to 60 hours and run on limited days. They are a travel experience rather than a practical holiday option.

## The four-night route, day by day

This is the trip we recommend to most first-time visitors from Chennai. It follows our [four-night Shillong, Cherrapunji, Dawki and Mawlynnong itinerary](/packages/meghalaya-classic-4n/), from ₹16,500 per person.

**Day 1: Guwahati to Shillong.** Land in Guwahati and drive about 3 hours up to [Shillong](/places/shillong/), stopping at the [Umiam lake](/places/umiam-lake/) viewpoint. Evening walk around Police Bazar.

**Day 2: Shillong Peak, Elephant Falls and Sohra.** Morning views from Shillong Peak at about 1,965 m, a short walk down at Elephant Falls, then on to [Sohra](/places/sohra/) for the night, with Mawkdok valley and the Seven Sisters falls along the way.

## Rules worth knowing before you land

Meghalaya is one of the simplest states in the Northeast for paperwork, but a few rules catch first-time visitors.

- **No permit, no state entry fee.** Indian and foreign visitors do not need an Inner Line Permit for Meghalaya.
- **Guest registration.** Since June 2025, every hotel, homestay and resort must register guests on the state tourism app. Keep photo ID for every adult handy at check-in. The state has also announced plans for registration at entry points; check current status before you travel.
- **No self-drive rentals.** Renting private cars to tourists for self-drive is not allowed, so book a car with a local driver.
- **Small gate fees in cash.** Village sights such as Mawlynnong, Nohkalikai and Mawsmai charge small fees, typically under ₹100 per person, plus parking.
- **Boats priced per boat.** At Dawki, fares are per boat rather than per person. Agree the fare at the ghat before you step in.
- **Plastic.** Several villages restrict single-use plastic. Carry a steel bottle and take rubbish back with you.

## Making the Kolkata connection work

Because Chennai has fewer non-stops to Guwahati than Delhi or Mumbai, many of our Chennai guests fly via Kolkata. Done right, it costs only a few extra hours.

Choose a connection with at least 90 minutes between flights if both are on one ticket, and three hours or more if you book them separately. Kolkata to Guwahati is only about 1 hour 15 minutes in the air, with flights through the day. A few flights a week also go from Kolkata straight to Shillong's small airport at Umroi; they save the Guwahati drive but are weather-sensitive, so keep a road plan as a backup.

A useful trick for the return: if your last night is in Shillong and the non-stop home leaves Guwahati early, a later departure via Kolkata can save you a pre-dawn start down the hill.

## Families and senior travellers

Chennai families often travel with grandparents, and Meghalaya is one of the easier hill trips in India for them. The highest point on the usual route is Shillong Peak, below 2,000 m, so altitude sickness is not a concern. Most drives are under three hours.

## Eating well as a Chennai traveller

Food is one of the most common questions we get from Chennai. The short answer: you will eat well in the towns and simply in the villages.

Shillong and Guwahati have plenty of vegetarian restaurants serving North Indian thalis, and a few places serve dosa and idli. Rice is the staple everywhere, which most Chennai travellers find reassuring. For the adventurous, Shillong's Khasi dishes such as jadoh (rice cooked with meat) and Assamese fish curries are worth trying; our [Shillong cafes and food guide](/guides/shillong-cafes-and-food/) lists where to go.

In Sohra, Mawlynnong and along the Dawki road, menus shrink to rice, dal, vegetables, omelettes and Maggi. Carry curd rice podi, pickle, snacks or ready-to-eat packs if your family prefers familiar tastes on long days. Tell your hotel in advance about any Jain, no-onion-garlic or strictly vegetarian needs.

## Packing from a warm city

Chennai travellers are the most likely to underpack for the cold. From November to February, Shillong's daytime can feel pleasant at 15 to 18°C but average lows are about 6 to 7°C, colder on clear nights, and hotel rooms without heaters feel colder than the number suggests. Pack a proper jacket, a sweater, thermals, socks and a woollen cap. In every season carry a rain jacket and shoes with grip; Sohra can rain even in the dry months. Add motion-sickness tablets for the winding roads.
