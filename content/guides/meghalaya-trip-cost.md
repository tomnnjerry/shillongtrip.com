The single biggest mistake we see in Meghalaya budgets is pricing the hotel and forgetting the car. In the Khasi Hills you cannot link Shillong, Sohra and Dawki on public transport in one day, so a hired vehicle is usually the second-largest line after rooms. Get those two right and the rest of the maths is small change.

Below is how the Shillong Trip Travel Desk prices a Meghalaya trip in 2026: rate cards for rooms and vehicles, the fees that catch people out, and three worked examples you can copy. Every number is an indicative range, not a quote, and all are for Indian travellers, twin sharing, starting and ending in Guwahati.

## What drives the cost

A standard 5-day, 4-night loop — two nights in [Shillong](/places/shillong/), one or two in [Sohra (Cherrapunji)](/places/sohra/), a day out to [Dawki and the Umngot River](/places/dawki-umngot/) — breaks down roughly like this for a couple in standard hotels:

| Cost line | Share of trip | What moves it |
|---|---|---|
| Hotels (4 nights) | 30–40% | Tier, view, dates, Sohra vs Shillong |
| Private car (5 days incl. transfers) | 30–35% | Vehicle type, route length, season |
| Food | 20–25% | Hotel restaurants vs local eateries |
| Entry fees, boats, guides | 5–8% | Dawki boat, trek guide, caves |
| Tips and extras | 2–5% | Driver tip, parking, rain gear |

Flights or trains to Guwahati sit outside this table because they depend on where you start. As a rough 2026 guide, one-way economy fares booked four to six weeks out tend to land between ₹4,000 and ₹12,000 from the metros, higher around festivals.

## Food, fees and small spends

Meals are cheap unless every one is eaten in a resort. A plate of rice, dal and pork or chicken at a Khasi eatery in Shillong or Sohra costs ₹150–300; a café meal in Laitumkhrah or Police Bazar ₹400–800; a resort dinner ₹900–2,000 per head.

Budget these per person per day:

- Budget: ₹500–800
- Standard: ₹900–1,500
- Premium: ₹1,500–2,500
- Luxury: ₹2,500–4,000 (often partly included in room rates)

Entry fees are small but constant. Most falls, caves, viewpoints and villages charge a small fee, typically under ₹100 per person, plus parking. There is no statewide tourist fee to enter Meghalaya. The bigger items:

- **Umngot boat at Dawki or Shnongpdeng**: priced per boat, not per person, so a full boat is cheapest; agree the fare at the ghat before you step in
- **Nongriat trek**: small village fees; a local guide is optional and costs a few hundred to ₹1,000
- **Adventure add-ons** at Shnongpdeng, such as kayaking or ziplining: ₹300–1,500 each
- **Rain gear**: a ₹200–400 poncho from Police Bazar beats a soaked day

All of these are set by village councils or local operators and change with little notice, so check current status before you travel.

## Rules that affect your budget

Two Meghalaya rules introduced in 2025 change how you plan. First, renting private cars to tourists for self-drive is prohibited, so the choice is a hired car with a driver or shared taxis; do not budget for a self-drive rental. Second, every hotel, homestay and resort must register guests on the state tourism app, which is one more reason to book stays that are properly listed rather than informal rooms. Neither rule adds a fee. Check current status before you travel, as registration at entry points has also been planned.

## Getting to Guwahati for less

The trip starts before Meghalaya. Guwahati (GAU) is the gateway for almost everyone, with direct flights from Delhi, Kolkata, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad and several smaller cities. Shillong's own airport at Umroi, about 30 km from town, has a small number of flights on smaller aircraft; fares are often higher and weather cancellations are more common than at Guwahati, so we treat it as a bonus, not a plan.

By rail, Guwahati station is served by Rajdhani and superfast trains from Delhi, Kolkata, Mumbai, Bengaluru and Chennai. A 3AC berth from Kolkata costs far less than a flight and saves a hotel night if you take an overnight train. From Delhi or the south the journey runs 28 to 50 hours, which eats two days of leave.

Two habits cut transport costs more than anything else: fly into Guwahati before 1 pm so you reach Shillong the same evening without an extra hotel night, and fly out after 2 pm so the last morning is not wasted.

## Costs people forget

Most budgets we are sent are 10–15% short, and it is nearly always the same lines:

- **The driver's night charge.** If the car stays in Sohra or Dawki overnight, some drivers add ₹300–500 for their own stay and food. Ask up front.
- **Early starts and late finishes.** A day rate usually covers about 10 hours or a fixed route. Going to Laitlum for sunrise or returning late from Mawlynnong can add a few hundred rupees.
- **Heating.** Budget rooms in Shillong and Sohra can charge ₹200–400 a night for a room heater from November to February.
- **Parking at Police Bazar and Ward's Lake.** Small sums, but they add up across five days.
- **Airport pick-up after dark.** A late arrival into Guwahati often means an extra night in the city, ₹1,800–4,000 for a decent room near the airport or station.
- **Rain days.** In the monsoon, a washed-out day still costs you the car and the room. Build in one spare day from June to September.

## What a package quote should include

When you compare quotes, including ours, check that each one lists the same things. A fair Meghalaya package quote names the hotels or at least the tier and area, states the vehicle type and whether it is exclusive to you, includes Guwahati airport or station pick-up and drop, and says whether breakfast only or breakfast and dinner are included. Entry fees, boat rides, guides and lunch are normally excluded, and that is fine as long as it is written down. If one quote is ₹5,000 cheaper per person, the difference is usually a smaller car, a hotel outside the town centre, or transfers missing.

## When prices go up or down

Meghalaya has a clear rate calendar. The peak weeks for hotels are Durga Puja and the October holidays, the fortnight from Christmas to New Year, and long weekends; expect room rates 20–40% above the figures in our tables and book 6–8 weeks ahead. Car rates rise less, maybe ₹200–500 a day.

June to August is the other end. Heavy rain in Sohra and Mawsynram means fewer visitors and many hotels quietly discount; the falls are at their fullest, but Dawki's water turns muddy and boats may stop. ## Ways to save without spoiling the trip

- Travel as five or six and share one Innova; per-head car cost roughly halves.
- Stay two nights in Sohra instead of doing it as a day trip from Shillong; you save a 110 km round drive and see the falls in morning light.
- Eat lunch where drivers eat. Khasi rice-and-meat plates are the best-value food in the state.
- Skip the paid "view points" set up on private land along the Sohra road; the main public viewpoints cover the same valleys.
- Carry cash. Village fees, homestays and boats rarely take cards, and ATMs outside Shillong can run dry.
- Book the car and hotels together for peak weeks. In late December, drivers with free dates are as scarce as rooms, and last-minute cars cost more.
- Travel midweek if you can. Shillong fills with weekend visitors from Guwahati, and Sunday-night rates in Sohra are often lower than Saturday's.
