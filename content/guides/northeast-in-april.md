April is the one month when you can ride a jeep through Kaziranga in the morning, watch Bihu dancers in the evening, and still find snow on the road to Tawang a few days later. It sits between the dry winter and the monsoon, and it is one of our favourite months to plan a longer Northeast trip.

The catches are heat on the Assam plains, evening thunderstorms, and a narrowing Kaziranga window as May approaches. Here is how April plays out across the region.

## April weather across the region

Long-term averages, rounded and approximate. Pre-monsoon storms make April weather more changeable than December's.

| Place | Day high (approx.) | Night low (approx.) | April rain (approx.) | Feel |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Guwahati | 31 °C | 20 °C | about 180 mm | Warm, humid, evening storms |
| Kaziranga (Tezpur normals) | 30 °C | 20 °C | about 170 mm | Hot afternoons, easy mornings |
| Shillong | 23 °C | 14 °C | about 130 mm | Mild, bright mornings |
| Sohra (Cherrapunji) | 23 °C | 14 °C | about 790 mm | Heavy showers returning |
| Tawang (modelled, approximate) | 10–14 °C | around 0 °C | about 250 mm | Cool and clear, cold nights |

Two things stand out. Sohra's April rainfall is already about six times Shillong's, a sign the monsoon is building in the south of Meghalaya. And the Assam plains are noticeably warmer than in March, so the hills feel like a relief.

## Bordoisila, the April storms

Assam calls the spring thunderstorms Bordoisila. They build on hot afternoons and break in the late afternoon or evening with strong wind, hail on some days and a sharp drop in temperature. They usually pass within an hour or two.

What this means for planning:

- do viewpoints, safaris and long walks in the morning
- keep the late afternoon for drives, cafes or indoor stops
- avoid river crossings and boat rides when a storm is building
- expect the occasional flight delay at Guwahati on stormy evenings

## Rongali Bihu in Assam

Rongali Bihu, also called Bohag Bihu, is the Assamese New Year and the state's biggest festival. It marks the start of the sowing season.

- **Goru Bihu, 14 April.** Cattle are bathed in rivers and ponds and honoured.
- **Manuh Bihu, 15 April.** New Year's Day: new clothes, visits to elders, gamusa gifts.
- **The days after.** Husori groups go house to house singing, and community stages (Bihutoli) host dance and music for about a week.

[Guwahati](/places/guwahati/) has the largest stage events. Upper Assam, including Sivasagar, Jorhat and [Majuli](/places/majuli/), gives a more village-level experience. Our [Rongali Bihu in Assam package](/packages/bihu-assam-5n/) is built around this week. Stage schedules are announced locally, so check current status before you travel.

Bihu week also means Assamese families travel and celebrate at home. Some shops and offices close on 14–15 April, and local transport is busier.

## Shad Suk Mynsiem in Shillong

The Khasi spring festival, Shad Suk Mynsiem, is held in [Shillong](/places/shillong/) in April, usually over three days at the Weiking ground in Jaiaw. Unmarried women in traditional silk and gold ornaments dance in the centre with small, measured steps, while men with swords and fly-whisks circle around them to drums and pipes. It is a thanksgiving for the harvest and a welcome to the sowing season.

Dates are set each year by the organisers. If you want to see it, confirm the dates in March and plan your Shillong nights around them.

## Other spring festivals around April

Bihu and Shad Suk Mynsiem are the two big ones, but several communities mark the new year or the sowing season around the same time.

| Festival | Community and place | Usual timing | Worth knowing |

| Bwisagu | Bodo, western Assam | Mid-April, alongside Bihu | Celebrated in Bodoland districts near Manas |
| Shad Suk Mynsiem | Khasi, Shillong | April, usually three days | Held at the Weiking ground in Jaiaw |

Dates for community festivals are announced locally and can shift by a few days year to year. Check current status before you travel, and ask us to confirm the programme before you lock in flights.

## Who April suits

April works well for:

- **Wildlife travellers** who want Kaziranga with short grass and fewer vehicles.
- **Culture seekers** timing a trip to Bihu or Shad Suk Mynsiem.
- **Couples and friends** heading to Tawang for cool, bright days and a chance of snow at Sela without deep-winter cold.
- **Travellers who want a long, mixed trip** covering plains and hills in one go.

It suits these travellers less:

- **Families who dislike heat.** Assam afternoons in the low 30s °C, with humidity, can be tiring for small children and seniors.
- **Anyone who wants full Meghalaya waterfalls.** They are only starting to refill; October is better.
- **Travellers with fixed plans and no flexibility** for a thunderstorm afternoon.

## Getting around in April

Road distances do not change with the season, but April traffic and storms do affect timings.

- **Guwahati to Kaziranga:** about 195 km, 4.5–5.5 hours. Start early to avoid the midday heat and afternoon storms.
- **Kaziranga to Shillong:** about 5–6 hours via Guwahati's edge and NH6.
- **Guwahati to Shillong:** about 100 km from the city, around 3 hours, more on stormy evenings.
- **Guwahati to Tawang:** a two-day drive via Bhalukpong and Dirang, crossing Sela on day two.

During Bihu week, city traffic in Guwahati is heavier around stage venues in the evening, and some local taxis take the main days off. We confirm drivers well in advance for 14–15 April.

## Kaziranga's last weeks

[Kaziranga](/places/kaziranga/) is usually in its final month of the season in April, with the park closing to tourists around the start of May for the monsoon. The exact closing date is set each year, and early floods can bring it forward, so check current status before you travel.

Why April is good for a safari:

- **Short grass.** Controlled burning in winter clears the tall elephant grass, and fresh shoots draw rhinos, buffaloes and deer into the open.
- **Water sources shrink,** so animals gather around the remaining wetlands.
- **Fewer vehicles** than in December or February.

The downsides are heat after 10 am and haze on some days. We book the earliest jeep or elephant safari and keep the afternoon for a short drive or rest. ## Tawang, Sela and Bum La in April

April is one of the two best windows for [Tawang](/places/tawang/), along with October and November. Days are cool and bright, and the road is more predictable than in deep winter.

- **[Sela Pass](/places/sela-pass/)** at about 4,170 m often still holds snow on the slopes in early April, and occasionally fresh snowfall. The Sela Tunnel keeps the main road more reliable.
- **Bum La** generally operates, with snow still possible. Permits are processed in Tawang and can be suspended for weather or army reasons.
- **Rhododendrons** start flowering at altitude through April and into May, depending on the year.
- **Nights** in Tawang still drop to around freezing, so heated rooms matter.

Indian visitors need an Arunachal ILP, and foreign nationals need a PAP; our [Arunachal ILP guide](/guides/arunachal-ilp-guide/) explains how. ## Meghalaya in April

Meghalaya in April is a mix of clear mornings and building storms.

**Shillong** is at its most pleasant: about 14–23 °C, fresh green on the pines after the dry months, and Shad Suk Mynsiem if your dates align.

**Sohra** is getting wet again. Waterfalls start to fill after the dry months, but views can fog over in the afternoon. Go early.

**The [Umngot at Dawki](/places/dawki-umngot/)** is usually still clear in the first half of April. Storms upstream can cloud it for a day or two, and by late April and May it becomes less reliable. If the clear water is a must, go early in the month.

**Nongriat** is a good walk in April: the streams are starting to rise, and the steps are mostly dry in the mornings.

## Majuli and Upper Assam at Bihu

If Bihu is your reason to travel, Upper Assam is where it feels closest to its village roots. Majuli, the river island reached by ferry from Nimati Ghat near Jorhat, celebrates in its satras and Mising villages, and the island is still green before the monsoon floods. Sivasagar, the old Ahom capital, has its own Bihu stages near the tanks and monuments.

April is also a comfortable month for the ferry, with river levels still moderate. Ferry timings can change with water levels and storms, so leave the afternoon free on crossing days. Two nights in Majuli gives time for a satra visit, a mask-making village and an evening Bihu gathering without rushing.

Jorhat's tea gardens are in their first flush season around this time, and a garden walk in the cool of the morning pairs well with the island.

## Crowds, prices and booking

April is a shoulder month. Indian school summer holidays generally start in May, so April sees fewer family groups than late May and June, and fewer than October or December.

- **Bihu week in Assam:** book Guwahati, Jorhat and Majuli stays 4–6 weeks ahead.
- **Kaziranga:** weekday safaris are usually easy to get with 2–3 weeks' notice; weekends fill sooner.
- **Tawang:** April is a busy month for domestic travel in Arunachal. Book heated rooms 4–6 weeks out.
- **Shillong and Sohra:** 3–4 weeks is usually enough outside long weekends.

## Packing for April

- **Assam:** light cotton, a hat, sunscreen, insect repellent, a light rain shell.
- **Meghalaya:** a light jacket, a compact umbrella, shoes with good grip for wet steps.
- **Tawang and Sela:** thermal top, padded or down jacket, gloves, cap and sunglasses for glare off the snow.
- **Everywhere:** a refillable water bottle and oral rehydration sachets for hot days on the plains.

## How we'd plan it

For a first April trip, we like a 7-night loop that moves from plains to hills as the days warm up:

1. **Night 1, Guwahati.** Arrive, Kamakhya in the evening. If it is 14 or 15 April, catch a Bihu stage.
2. **Nights 2–3, Kaziranga.** Morning safaris on both days; afternoon tea-garden walk or rest.
3. **Nights 4–5, Shillong.** Via Guwahati, about 5–6 hours from Kaziranga. Shad Suk Mynsiem if the dates match, plus Laitlum and Elephant Falls.
4. **Night 6, Sohra.** Early start for viewpoints before the afternoon cloud.
5. **Night 7, Shillong or Guwahati.** Dawki on the way back if the water is clear.
