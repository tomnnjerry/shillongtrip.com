Most "Shillong sightseeing" lists are really lists of Meghalaya. Nohkalikai, Dawki and the root bridges are two to three hours away, not in the city. This guide sticks to what is genuinely in Shillong or within an hour of it, then adds the day trips at the end, clearly marked.

We measure every distance from Police Bazar, the centre of town. The city sits at about 1,525 m and needs no permit, though your hotel will register you on the state system. Our [Shillong page](/places/shillong/) has the basics on getting in, staying and eating.

## Shillong in three rings

| Ring | Distance from Police Bazar | Places | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| City centre | 0–5 km | Ward's Lake, Don Bosco Museum, cathedral, Iewduh, Lady Hydari Park, golf course | 1 day |
| Upper Shillong | 10–12 km | Shillong Peak, Elephant Falls | Half a day |
| Outer ring | 15–25 km | Umiam Lake, Laitlum, Mawphlang, Mawkdok, Sweet Falls | 1 day |
| Day trips | 55–100 km | Sohra, Mawlynnong and Dawki, Jowai and Nartiang | 1 day each |

## In the city

### 1. Ward's Lake and the Botanical Garden

**Altitude:** about 1,500 m · **Distance:** 1 km, 5 min · **Best months:** October to May · **Permit:** none, small entry ticket · **Time:** 1 hr

A horseshoe-shaped lake from the British period with a wooden bridge, paddle boats and a garden next door. Walk it at opening time; by mid-morning it fills with families.

**Tip:** combine it with a stroll along the old Secretariat roads nearby, lined with pines and colonial buildings.

### 2. Don Bosco Museum

**Altitude:** about 1,500 m · **Distance:** 4 km, 15–20 min · **Best months:** all year · **Permit:** none, entry ticket · **Time:** 2 hrs

Seven floors on the peoples of the Northeast, from Naga textiles to Khasi rituals, ending at a rooftop skywalk with a view over Mawlai. It is the best rainy-day stop in the city.

**Tip:** start at the top and walk down; the skywalk is best before the afternoon haze.

### 3. Cathedral of Mary Help of Christians

**Altitude:** about 1,500 m · **Distance:** 2 km, 10 min · **Best months:** all year · **Permit:** none · **Time:** 30 min

A tall blue-and-white cathedral in Laitumkhrah, known for its stained glass and Stations of the Cross in terracotta.

**Tip:** dress modestly and visit outside Sunday service times unless you want to attend.

### 4. Iewduh (Bara Bazar)

**Altitude:** about 1,500 m · **Distance:** 1.5 km, 10 min · **Best months:** all year · **Permit:** none · **Time:** 1–2 hrs

One of the oldest traditional markets in the Northeast, run largely by Khasi women. Spices, betel, bamboo baskets, dried fish, local tea and cloth are sold in lanes that change every few steps.

**Tip:** the market follows the eight-day Khasi week, so ask your hotel which day is the main market day.

### 5. Lady Hydari Park

**Altitude:** about 1,500 m · **Distance:** 2 km, 10 min · **Best months:** October to May · **Permit:** none, entry ticket · **Time:** 1 hr

A landscaped park with a small zoo and a pine garden. It is simple, but children like it after a morning in the car.

**Tip:** pair it with Ward's Lake on the same half day.

### 6. Shillong Golf Course

**Altitude:** about 1,500 m · **Distance:** 3 km, 10 min · **Best months:** October to April · **Permit:** none for walking the edges; green fees to play · **Time:** 1 hr

One of the oldest golf courses in India, laid in a pine-ringed valley. Non-golfers can walk the edges at dawn or dusk.

**Tip:** visiting players should call the club a day ahead about green fees and club hire.

## Upper Shillong

### 7. Shillong Peak

**Altitude:** 1,965 m · **Distance:** 10 km, 30 min · **Best months:** October to April · **Permit:** original photo ID for Indians at the Air Force gate; foreign nationals generally not allowed · **Time:** 1–1.5 hrs

The highest point around the city, with a wide view over Shillong on clear mornings. See [Shillong Peak](/places/shillong-peak/) for entry rules, and check current status before you travel.

**Tip:** leave the hotel by 8 am; mist often covers the view by 11.

### 8. Elephant Falls

**Altitude:** about 1,600 m · **Distance:** 12 km, 30 min · **Best months:** June to November · **Permit:** none, entry ticket · **Time:** 1 hr

Three tiers of water down a ferny gully, reached by steps. [Elephant Falls](/places/elephant-falls/) is thin in March and April and loud after the rains.

**Tip:** the lowest tier is the best photo; allow ten minutes for the climb back.

## The outer ring

### 9. Umiam Lake

**Altitude:** about 980 m · **Distance:** 15 km, 30 min · **Best months:** October to May · **Permit:** none, boating fees · **Time:** 1–2 hrs

The reservoir on the Guwahati road, with boating, kayaking and viewpoints over the water. [Umiam Lake](/places/umiam-lake/) works best as a stop on arrival or departure day.

**Tip:** the viewpoint on the highway gets crowded at 4 pm; the water sports complex is calmer.

### 10. Laitlum Canyons

**Altitude:** about 1,750 m · **Distance:** 22 km, 45 min · **Best months:** October to April · **Permit:** none, village parking fee · **Time:** 2–4 hrs

Grassy ridges falling into deep gorges, with a long stairway to Rasong village for walkers. [Laitlum](/places/laitlum-canyons/) is also the viewpoint we send foreign travellers to instead of Shillong Peak.

**Tip:** sunrise is clearer than sunset here; cloud rolls in by early afternoon.

### 11. Mawphlang Sacred Forest

**Altitude:** about 1,800 m · **Distance:** 25 km, 45–60 min · **Best months:** all year, driest October to April · **Permit:** none, guide compulsory · **Time:** 1.5–2 hrs

A community forest where nothing may be taken, not even a dry leaf. The guides at [Mawphlang](/places/mawphlang-sacred-forest/) explain Khasi rituals and the monoliths near the entrance.

**Tip:** the David Scott Trail, an old bridle path of about 14 km, starts close by if you want a longer walk.

### 12. Mawkdok Dympep viewpoint

**Altitude:** about 1,700 m · **Distance:** about 25 km, 45 min · **Best months:** October to April · **Permit:** none · **Time:** 30–45 min

The first big valley view on the Sohra road, with a zipline across the gorge.

**Tip:** stop on your way to Sohra rather than as a separate trip.

### 13. Sweet Falls

**Altitude:** about 1,500 m · **Distance:** about 6 km, 20 min · **Best months:** June to October · **Permit:** none · **Time:** 45 min

A slender, high fall near Happy Valley, quieter than Elephant Falls.

**Tip:** stay behind the barriers; the edges are unfenced in places and have been dangerous.

## Day trips from Shillong

These need a full day each, and most people are better off sleeping near them.

### 14. Sohra (Cherrapunji)

**Altitude:** about 1,480 m · **Distance:** 55 km, 1.5–2 hrs · **Best months:** October to April, or June to September for rain · **Permit:** none · **Time:** 1 day or 1 night

The falls, caves and plateau of [Sohra](/places/sohra/), including [Nohkalikai Falls](/places/nohkalikai-falls/).

**Tip:** leave by 7:30 am to beat the weekend jam at the Sohra viewpoints.

### 15. Mawlynnong

**Altitude:** roughly 800 m · **Distance:** about 80 km, 2.5–3 hrs · **Best months:** October to April · **Permit:** none, village fees · **Time:** half a day

The tidy village of [Mawlynnong](/places/mawlynnong/), with the Riwai root bridge nearby.

**Tip:** go on a weekday; weekends are crowded.

### 16. Dawki and the Umngot River

**Altitude:** under 100 m · **Distance:** about 85–95 km, 2.5–3 hrs · **Best months:** November to April · **Permit:** none, boat fee · **Time:** half a day

Boats on clear green water at the Bangladesh border. [Dawki](/places/dawki-umngot/) pairs with Mawlynnong on the same road.

**Tip:** in the monsoon the river is brown; save it for winter.

### 17. Jowai and Nartiang

**Altitude:** about 1,300–1,380 m · **Distance:** 64 km, 2 hrs · **Best months:** October to April · **Permit:** none · **Time:** 1 day

The Jaintia Hills town of [Jowai](/places/jowai/) and the standing stones of [Nartiang](/places/nartiang-monoliths/).

**Tip:** add Thadlaskein lake on the way; it is right on the highway.

### 18. Krang Suri Falls

**Altitude:** about 800 m · **Distance:** about 90–100 km, 3 hrs · **Best months:** October to May · **Permit:** none, entry fee · **Time:** 1 day

A blue pool under a curtain fall. It can close in heavy rain, so check current status before you travel.

**Tip:** life jackets are for hire at the pool; use one.

## Getting around the city

Shillong's local taxis are small hatchbacks that run shared on fixed routes, picking up and dropping along the way. They are cheap for short hops, such as Police Bazar to Laitumkhrah, but useless for sightseeing because they will not wait. For a day of sights, hire a full cab with a driver and agree the route and the finishing time before you set off. Self-drive rentals are not an option: Meghalaya has prohibited renting private vehicles to tourists for self-drive since June 2025. A half-day covering Upper Shillong and the city usually costs less than a full day to Laitlum and Mawphlang.

Walking works in the centre. Police Bazar, Ward's Lake, the State Central Library area and the old Secretariat roads are all within 15 minutes of each other on foot, but the hills are steep and pavements narrow. Parking is scarce around Police Bazar in the evening, which is one more reason to leave the car at the hotel and walk to dinner.

Traffic is the one thing that catches visitors out. The school run around 8:30–9:30 am and the office rush around 4–5 pm can turn a 3 km trip into 40 minutes. Do Upper Shillong before 9 or after 10, and aim to be back in town before the evening jam.

## Where to base yourself

- **Police Bazar:** central, walkable, most mid-range hotels, busy and noisy until 8 pm
- **Laitumkhrah:** cafes, the cathedral and live music, a little quieter at night
- **Upper Shillong and the Sohra road:** pine forest and cooler air, but you need the car for everything
- **Umiam Lake:** a resort stay by the water, 30 minutes from town, good for a slow first or last night

## If it rains all day

Rain is common in Shillong in every month, not just the monsoon. When the viewpoints are in cloud, switch the day around. Spend the morning in the Don Bosco Museum and the afternoon at the Capt. Williamson Sangma State Museum or the Air Force Museum in Upper Shillong. Eat a long lunch of jadoh and tungrymbai, browse the covered sections of Iewduh and end with music in a Laitumkhrah cafe. Keep Laitlum and Shillong Peak for the next clear morning; they are close enough to slot back in at short notice.

## Shillong through the year

October and November bring clear skies, strong falls and, in November, cherry blossom on some streets. December and January are cold, with nights that can dip to around 3–5 °C, and they have the clearest views. February to April warms up, with spring haze by April. May is warm and busy with school-holiday traffic from the plains. June to September is monsoon: every fall is full, cloud sits on the ridges and the city smells of wet pine.
